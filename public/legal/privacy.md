# Privacy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This page says exactly what this site records about you, why, and who else is involved. The short version: you do not need an account, we do not store your name or your IP address, and we never see your card details.

## Who is responsible

Khizar Alam runs this site. Contact for anything about your data: {{email}}.

## What is stored when you browse

**A random id in your browser.** The first time you visit, your browser makes a random id and keeps it in its local storage. It is how the site knows which hearts are yours. Our database never stores that id itself, only a scrambled (salted and hashed) version of it that cannot be turned back.

**What you did.** When you open an animation, download or copy one, or press a heart, we record that it happened, for which animation, and when. A visitor counts once per animation per day.

**Roughly where and how.** With each of those we record:

- your country and city, as estimated by our hosting provider from your connection
- whether you are on a phone, a tablet or a desktop
- the website you came from, reduced to its name (for example youtube.com), if your browser sends it

**What is not stored.** Your name, your email (unless you buy something, see below), your IP address. To stop flooding, a scrambled value made from your IP is kept for one day; it changes every day and cannot be turned back into an IP.

**In your browser only.** Your hearts, your White or Black choice, and a note of any purchase's download address are kept in your browser's local storage so they work at once. Clearing your browser's site data removes them. The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

## What is stored when you buy

Payments are handled by **Paddle**, the seller of record. You type your card and billing details into Paddle's checkout, not into this site. We never see or store your card number.

After a payment Paddle tells us: the transaction id, which animation it was for, the amount and currency, and your email address. We keep those so we can give you your files, answer support requests and handle refunds.

## Why we record this

- to show download and heart counts
- to understand which animations people like and where visitors come from
- to deliver what you bought and support you afterwards
- to keep the site from being abused

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## How long it is kept

Activity records (views, downloads, hearts) are kept while the site runs, in the scrambled form described above. Purchase records are kept for as long as the law requires for sales records.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete what is in your browser:** clear this site's data in your browser settings. Your hearts stop being linked to you.
- **Ask what we hold or ask us to delete it:** write to {{email}}. For browsing data we will need the random id from your browser's storage to find your records. For purchases, write from the email you paid with.
- Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object, to correct data, or to complain to your data protection authority.

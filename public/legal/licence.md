# Licence

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This page says what you may do with the animations from this site, in plain words.

## Paid animations: pay once, use it anywhere

When you buy an animation you get a licence to use it for as long as you like. One payment. No subscription, no royalties, no limit on how many products or how many units.

**You may**

- use it in your own projects, personal or commercial
- put it on products you sell: gadgets, kits, merchandise, displays, installations
- show it in videos, streams, posts and demos, including ones that earn money
- change it: resize it, recolour it, edit the code, combine it with your own work
- use it for client work, where the client receives it as part of what you built

**You may not**

- resell, share or give away the files themselves, changed or not, so that others get the animation without buying it. This includes uploading them to a repository, a download site, or a pack of animations
- share your download page's address
- sell it as your own animation, or claim you made it

The licence is for you, or for your company if you bought it for work. A refund ends the licence.

## Free animations

The free animations may be downloaded, used and changed in personal and commercial projects at no cost. Please do not sell the files themselves or repackage them as your own collection.

## Characters and names that belong to others

Some animations are fan art of characters, names or logos owned by other people or companies. The licence above covers the animation work and code made for this site. It cannot give you rights in someone else's character or trademark. If you plan to sell a product that shows one, check that you are allowed to use that character or mark where you sell.

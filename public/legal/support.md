## Buying and downloading

**I bought an animation. Where are my files?**
Straight after paying you land on your download page. It lists every file with a download button.

**I closed or refreshed the page. Do I have to pay again?**
No. You never pay twice for something you bought. Your download page has its own address, and that address keeps working. Open it again and it gives you fresh download links. Refreshing is fine too: it simply makes new links.

**The download buttons stopped working.**
Each link works for 10 minutes, for safety. Reload your download page and the buttons work again.

**How do I get back to my download page later?**
Three ways:

1. Bookmark the page when you land on it, or use the "Copy this page's address" button and save it.
2. On the same browser you bought with, open the animation's page. It shows "You own this. Get your files".
3. If you have lost the address and changed browser, write to {{email}} from the email you paid with, or attach your Paddle receipt. You will get your address back.

**The payment went through but the page says "Still waiting".**
The confirmation from the payment service can take a minute. Reload the page. You will not be charged twice. If it still waits after a few minutes, write in with your receipt.

**Can I download again on another computer?**
Yes. Open your download page's address on that computer.

**Can I use what I bought in a product I sell?**
Yes. See the [licence](/licence).

**Can I get a refund?**
See [refunds](/refunds).

## Getting an animation onto a board

Every animation comes with a step by step guide: wiring, Arduino IDE set up, upload, and what to do when the screen stays dark. Free animations show their guide on their page. Paid ones include it in the download.

The three most common fixes:

- **Upload fails with "Wrong boot mode":** hold BOOT, tap RST, let go of BOOT, then upload again.
- **Screen stays black after a good upload:** press RST once. If it is still black, swap the SDA and SCL wires.
- **The board appears on the PC and vanishes:** try another USB cable. Many cables only charge.

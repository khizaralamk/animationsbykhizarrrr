# Refunds

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Paid animations are digital files that you can download the moment you pay. Because of that, a purchase cannot be "returned" the way a physical thing can. This page says when you get your money back anyway.

## You get a refund when

- **It does not work as described.** If a paid animation does not do what its page says on the hardware its page names, write to {{email}} within **14 days** of buying. We will first try to fix it with you. If it cannot be made to work, you get a full refund.
- **You were charged twice** for the same animation. The second charge is refunded in full, whenever you notice.
- **You paid and never got the files**, and support could not get them to you within a reasonable time.

## You do not get a refund when

- you changed your mind after downloading
- you bought it for hardware its page does not list (for example a different screen size)
- more than 14 days have passed and the files worked as described

## How to ask

Write to **{{email}}** with the email you paid with, or the receipt Paddle sent you, and a short description of the problem. You will get an answer within two working days.

## How the money comes back

Payments are handled by Paddle, the seller of record. An approved refund goes back through Paddle to the same payment method you used. It usually shows up within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank.

## After a refund

Your download page for that purchase closes and the licence for that animation ends. Please delete the files.

## Free animations

Free animations cost nothing, so there is nothing to refund. If one does not work, [support](/support) will still help.

## Your legal rights

Nothing here takes away rights you have under the consumer law of the place you live.

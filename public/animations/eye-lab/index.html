Clowdbot Eye Lab

A pixel-exact preview of the 128 × 64 OLED. The eye on the side you look toward narrows and the other widens, so the glance reads like a head turning. It runs at the real board's frame rate, about 18 frames a second, so what you see here is what the glass will do. Pick an emotion to change the eye shape: happy and joy arch into smiles, love turns the eyes into beating hearts.

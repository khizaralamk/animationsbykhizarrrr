The Boogley

A dancing character traced from a 15 second video, one frame every tenth of a second. The character was cut out of the green screen, the drawn lines were kept, and every frame was shrunk to the screen. Pick a style, set the speed, and copy the frames for your board.

Area

Filled lights the whole character and leaves the drawn lines dark, which reads best on a real screen. Outline keeps just the edge and the lines. On a two colour module the ears sit in the yellow rows unless you pick Cyan rows only.

Speed

10 (as filmed)

The video was sampled at 10 frames a second, so 10 plays it at its real speed. Bounce plays forward then backward instead of jumping back to the start.

Put it on your board

1

Tune it here

Pick a style, the area and the speed. What plays on the screen above is what the board will play.

2

Copy the sketch

Press the red button. It copies the complete Arduino program, all 151 frames included, in the style and speed you picked.

3

Paste and upload

In the Arduino IDE make a new sketch, select all, paste, and press Upload. The guide in this folder has every click.


      
      
The complete sketch is about 800 KB of text because the frames are inside it, so the paste takes a moment. On the board it uses about 460 KB of flash, which an ESP32 has plenty of.

      
You need: an ESP32 DevKit board, a 0.96 inch I2C OLED, four jumper wires, a USB cable and the free Arduino IDE with the ESP32 boards and the Adafruit SSD1306 library installed. Wiring is GND to GND, VCC to 3V3, SCL to pin 26, SDA to pin 25. The full steps are in guide.md next to this file.

    

  


  

    
The Boogley is one HTML file. All 151 frames and the Arduino sketch live inside it, so it works offline and you can keep a copy.

    
Files in this folder: the-boogley.html (this page), guide.md (how to put it on an ESP32), BoogleyDance_OneFile/ (the whole program in one file), BoogleyDance/ (the same program in two files), frames/ (the 151 pictures cut from the video).

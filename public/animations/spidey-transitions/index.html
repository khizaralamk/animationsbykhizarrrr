Three spider emblems traced into real 1 bit pixels and shown on a simulated 0.96 inch OLED. Pick a spider, turn it, spin it, or run the glitch show. When it looks right, copy the settings into the free ESP32 sketch and your board will draw exactly what you see here.

Put it on your board

1

Tune it here

Pick a spider, a size, a line weight and the show timing. What you see on the screen above is what the board will draw.

2

Copy the sketch

Press the red button. It copies the complete Arduino program, spiders included, with your settings already filled in.

3

Paste and upload

In the Arduino IDE make a new sketch, select all, paste, and press Upload. That is the whole job. The guide in this folder has every click.


      
      
The settings lines above are for people who already have SpiderShow.ino open: replace the matching lines near the top. Everyone else can use the red button and paste the whole thing into an empty sketch.

      
You need: an ESP32 DevKit board, a 0.96 inch I2C OLED, four jumper wires, a USB cable and the free Arduino IDE with the ESP32 boards and the Adafruit SSD1306 library installed. Wiring is GND to GND, VCC to 3V3, SCL to pin 26, SDA to pin 25. The full steps are in guide.md next to this file.
